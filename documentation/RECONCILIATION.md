# Reconciliation with the submitted manuscript

The original workbook contains 35 Scopus, 18 IEEE, and 30 WoS candidate records (83 in total). Its status fields mark 26, 17, and 23 records as INCLUDED, respectively: 66 included labels, not the manuscript's 58 retained publications. The original manuscript retains 26 Scopus, 14 IEEE, and 18 WoS publications.

Differences preserved and documented:

- IEEE original IDs A01–A18 are local to that sheet. The manuscript uses B01–B18. Three records marked INCLUDED in the original IEEE sheet (original A15–A17, manuscript B15–B17) are explicitly excluded in the manuscript. The manuscript decisions are shown in the derived index without overwriting the original labels.
- WoS W08, W12, W13, W22, and W25 are marked INCLUDED in the original data but are absent from the final WoS table. Their titles correspond to retained IEEE publications B06, B07, B14, B05, and B13. This reconciles the difference between 23 original WoS included labels and 18 final WoS publications. It does not prove which historical deduplication pass removed each record.
- WoS W02, W15, W16, W17, and W23 are marked duplicate exclusions in the original data. Their titles correspond to A34, A29, A09, A05, and A08.
- WoS W29 and W30 are excluded in the original data but are absent from the manuscript's 13 non-duplicate exclusions. Their reasons are retained in the original records and flagged as NOT_IN_MANUSCRIPT_TABLES. Their relationship to the reported seven metadata duplicates cannot be reconstructed from the supplied files.
- The manuscript reports 83 candidates minus seven metadata duplicates = 76 full-text records, then 18 exclusions = 58 retained publications. The available candidate sheets do not uniquely reconstruct the allocation of every record to these two removal stages. This gap is exposed, not filled with invented decisions.

Bibliographic metadata in the derived 58-publication index follows the supplied BibTeX, while original candidate records are preserved. A26 has year 2025 in the supplied BibTeX, but the publisher’s final record confirms 2026, volume 86, issue 3, citation locator 11 (https://www.techscience.com/cmc/v86n3/65509). The derived index uses 2026; the original workbook and its metadata remain unchanged. Titles and author metadata in some historical WoS rows are abbreviated or anonymized; the index is not a wholesale correction of the original workbook.

Counts of venue forms use BibTeX entry types for the same 58 IDs: 32 inproceedings, 23 article, and three incollection entries. Publication format (full/short paper) was not consistently recorded. Survey classification is inherited from the original manuscript; this release does not reclassify study eligibility or research design.
